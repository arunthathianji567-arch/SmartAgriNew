Our Approach

A smarter way to understand agriculture

AgriCore AIoT connects agricultural sensors with an ESP32 and a web-based monitoring platform.

The system helps visualize environmental conditions and supports automated irrigation based on soil moisture.

  • Real-time ESP32 sensor monitoring
  • Soil moisture analysis
  • Temperature and humidity monitoring
  • Water-level distance monitoring
  • Automatic pump control
